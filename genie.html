Genie — the personal story companion

A conversational companion that helps readers find their next story

AI Conversational UI Personalization Concept

Overview

Genie is a personal story companion — a dedicated, AI-powered librarian built into the listening experience. Instead of scrolling endless shelves, readers can simply describe what they're in the mood for and Genie recommends the right book, explains why it fits, and answers questions about any title along the way.

The problem

Discovery is the hardest part of the reading experience. With hundreds of thousands of titles, finding the next great story is overwhelming — and generic charts rarely match what a specific reader actually wants in the moment. Many readers churn simply because they can't decide what to start next.

The idea

What if every reader had a knowledgeable friend who knew the entire catalogue? Genie turns discovery into a conversation. Ask for "a Scandinavian noir thriller for a first-time reader" and Genie returns a short, curated set with clear reasoning, inline previews, and the ability to dig deeper or save for later — all without leaving the chat.

Where it's going

This is an early draft. Next up: the design process, key flows, the conversational patterns behind the recommendations, and how Genie threads into the rest of the product.
